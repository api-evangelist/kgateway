DeepSeek R1 is a new model from Chinese startup DeepSeek released on January 20 with impressive reasoning ability which rivals (or beats) the existing leading reasoning models (ie, o1 from OpenAI). It is available from a few different providers but from my perspective, the most interesting thing is that it’s open source and is available in distilled form with similar performance (ie, distilled to Qwen or Llama models, which can be self-hosted). One question we see (from our users as well as the broader public) is “is DeepSeek safe?”.
