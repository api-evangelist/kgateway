When deploying workloads that rely on large language models (LLMs) in production, an important, yet often overlooked, challenge is LLM reliability. Models can go offline for all sorts of reasons: infrastructure hiccups, resource constraints, or version rollouts gone awry. When that happens, your AI-powered workloads are left with no response.
